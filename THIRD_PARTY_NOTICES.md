# Third-party sources and attribution

The spacecraft engineering telemetry is from ESA's Rosetta mission, dataset `RO-X-HK-3-RWL-V1.0`, distributed through the NASA PDS Small Bodies Node. The manifest preserves the exact product URLs and byte identities. The downloaded PDS labels preserve the original product metadata. Source data remain subject to the archive's own terms and attribution requirements. The repository's MIT license applies to authored code and documentation.

The operations reference is Federico Zuiani, Francesco Castellini, Sabine Kielbassa, Carlos Bielsa and Juan Manuel Garcia, *Rosetta Command and Monitoring Operations for Philae Landing*, 2015 International Symposium on Space Flight Dynamics, section 2.3. The paper is linked and paraphrased, with its figures and full text retained at the original host. [Full paper](https://issfd.org/2015/files/downloads/papers/098_Castellini.pdf)

The historical scripts and analysis were prepared in Ahmet's research workspace with AI assistance. Ahmet remains responsible for verifying experiments, sources and any scientific conclusions or publication disclosures.
